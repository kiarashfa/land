Round 03: three pages, one atmosphere.

You chose three ideas and gave each a page. Quicksilver is the home, with a room around it now instead of a void. The Trophy Shelf became a saleroom where each project is a lot, in the order it was made. The Riso boxes became real glass cases with small living rooms inside, one family at a time.

These pages are heavy on purpose. Open them on a computer with a good graphics card first, then on your phone. The thumbnails come from a software renderer and look flatter than the real thing.

What ties the pages together

materials
Black lacquer, brass, walnut and glass, with the same molten "cooling in" when an object appears.
ambients
Atelier, Dusk and Travertine are built once and reused: the home offers all three, and the family pages use two of them.
figures
One rigged body, posed in code and dressed by a paint shader, like a model maker's figures.
  • HeardQuicksilver for home, in a few versions, and never floating in a void.
  • HeardThe chronology as a luxury auction, one item taking the stage at a time, true to the home's mood.
  • HeardFamilies as glass boxes with living people inside: realistic, never cartoonish.

Story
You sit at the back of a walnut-panelled saleroom. Each lot turns on the home's lacquer plinth under one spot, the board flips to its month, the auctioneer raises the gavel and a few paddles go up. The catalogue card gives the date, family and line; the screen shows the real site.
Timeline
A ruler along the bottom, one bead per project by its first commit; the quiet stretch before 2026 is folded. Scroll, use the arrows or tap a bead.
Any number

Watch out
The audience are dark, faceless figures on purpose: real people would look uncanny at this distance. The dates are first commits until you confirm them.
