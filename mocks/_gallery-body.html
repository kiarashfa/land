Round 04: mercury, everywhere.

Quicksilver won, so mercury now binds the whole site. On the home, the two words still converge into the name, and every emblem stays pure mercury, as in Round 02. Colour only arrives when you open a project. That is the moment the mercury blooms into the real object.

The home now opens behind a loading screen that reads your machine. Capable graphics cards get the Atelier; phones get a lighter Atelier; slow or software graphics get the Round 02 void. A switch in the header lets you change it.

The Atelier bug

found
Each softbox sat exactly on the front face of its frame. On real graphics cards the two surfaces z-fight (flicker in stripes); the software renderer I test with hid it.
fixed
The panels are separated. The Atelier is also rebuilt without the two risky parts: the mirror floor is now mirrored copies under translucent stone, and the softbox lights are plain spotlights. A guard pass stops any bad pixel spreading through the glow.
check
