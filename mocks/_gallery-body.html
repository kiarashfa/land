KiarashFaLanding site · Round 01 · World concepts · 9 mocks

Nine worlds for twenty-five side projects.

Each mock is a different answer to one question: what kind of place should hold everything you make for the joy of it? They differ in metaphor, mood and technology. They are taste mocks, so judge the world and the feeling, not the content or the controls.

Open each one on a computer and on your phone. Only the first ten names are real projects; the rest are placeholders, and tiers are my guesses. Screens rendered on a real GPU look better than the thumbnails, which come from a software renderer.

How to read the tech labels

three.js
A 3D library. It draws through WebGL (or WebGPU) for us, with materials, lights and shadows.
raw shader
Hand-written GPU code with no library. Tiny and very fast, best for 2D light, terrain or lens effects.
Canvas 2D
No GPU 3D at all. Drawn line by line, like an engraving. Lightest and most robust.
physics
A rigid-body engine (matter.js here; Rapier for 3D) moves things you can grab and throw.

Monomer

Twenty-five beads on one chain.

dark luxurytactilethree.jsverlet physics
Story
You are a polymer scientist, so your body of work becomes one macromolecule. Each project is a monomer, and the chain doubles as a necklace.
The 25
One bead per project. Material and size encode tier (gold flagships, then glass, onyx, pearl). Drag the chain; hover a bead for its name.
Project 26
One more monomer joins the chain.
Watch out
Glass is heavy on weak phones and needs a fallback material. Reading names is indirect, so it needs a plain index view beside it.
Open concept →drag the chain

Prism

One curiosity, twenty-five colours.

Story
White light (your curiosity) enters a prism and splits. Every project is one wavelength, and the index of projects is the spectrum.

Complications

My day job tells the time. These are my complications.

dark luxuryhorologyCanvas 2Dno WebGL
Story
In watchmaking, a complication is anything a watch does beyond telling the time. The PhD is the time; the side projects are the complications.

25 indices with applied gold batons (doubled for flagships). A blued-steel hand swings to the project you point at; the date window shows its number; sub-dials show year and family.

05

Latent

Everything I've made, embedded.

dark scientificthree.js48k GPU particles
Story
Your machine-learning side. Projects are clusters in an embedding space, and each cluster condenses into its emblem: a helix, streamlines, a coupe glass, Mars, the letter ñ. It could be real: embed your own descriptions and project them at build time.
The 25
Clusters with a legend. The cloud tours from emblem to emblem; hovering the legend holds one.
Project 26
A new cluster lands where its description falls, plus one emblem shape designed in its own round.
Watch out
Particle clouds are a popular look. The emblems and the real embedding are what make it yours.
Open concept →wait for the morph, hover the legend
06

Kinetic

Twenty-five things, each on its own rhythm.

light galleryplayfulthree.jsPBR + shadows
Story
A pendulum-wave sculpture. Each project swings at its own period, and together they fall in and out of step, the way side projects come and go.
The 25
One bob per project, in brass, chrome or black glass. Hover for a name; tap anywhere to release the wave again.
Project 26
Periods are computed from the count, so a 26th bob re-tunes the whole wave.

Bobs are small targets on a phone, so it needs a list beside it.

These can combine

Each concept is three separable layers: the world (metaphor), the design language (type, palette, material) and the engine. You asked for several views over one data set, and different worlds can serve different views. For example:

  • Home: Monomer, with the chain as the signature.
  • Chronology: Atlas, where the voyage line is literally time.
  • Families: Complications, with sub-dials per family.
  • Project pages: Cabinet plates as each project's generative emblem.

Next round narrows to two or three directions and goes deeper: typography, palette, a real project page, motion and sound.
