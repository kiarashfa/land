Round 02: more 3D, one object per project.

Built from your Round 01 feedback and your project READMEs. Every world here holds any number of projects, and none of them states a count. The finite devices (the prism) only ever show a curated set. Each project now has its own sculpture, and the worlds restyle those same objects in gold, chrome, paper or brass.

Open each mock on a computer and on your phone. Scroll, drag or point inside them; most respond. Screens rendered on a real GPU look better than these thumbnails, which come from a software renderer.

How to read the tech labels

three.js
A 3D library drawing through WebGL or WebGPU, with materials, lights and shadows.
raw shader
Hand-written GPU code with no library. Quicksilver is ray-marched entirely in one shader.
Canvas 2D
No GPU 3D. Riso Boxes simulates the whole print process in code, with one network request.

01

Shadow Play

Every project is a tangle until you see it from the right angle.

dark theatricalthree.jsinstanced rodsreal shadows
Story
A bundle of brass rods hangs in a dark room. As you scroll it turns, and at exactly one angle its shadow on the wall becomes the project's object. Then the rods fly apart and re-form as the next one.
Any number
One project at a time in sequence, with an index at the side. The tangle is computed from each object's silhouette, so new projects need no manual work.
Watch out
The rod count must drop on older phones. The reveal is strongest for objects with clear silhouettes.
Open →scroll slowly

Mobile

light gallerykinetic, calmthree.jsbalanced tree
Story
A Calder-style mobile hangs against a pale wall. Each family is a branch and each piece is a project's object. Moving across it makes a little wind; tapping a piece holds it still.
Any number
The tree is balanced by weight, so adding a project hangs one more piece and the whole thing finds its rest again. That is the scaling story told literally.
Watch out
With many pieces they get small, so it needs a zoom into a family branch.

08

Riso Boxes

your idea

Everything Kia has made, printed on one sheet.

light, printedCanvas 2D onlyone network requestsimulated risograph
Story
Each project is a cut-away isometric dollhouse room with someone doing the project's thing: cooking, mixing a drink, reading an atlas, tapping Morse, pushing the button in the Swan. The sheet prints in, then tours room by room. Drag, scroll or pinch to explore; double-click a room to visit it.
Print
Four spot inks, each halftoned at its own screen angle with its own misregistration, overprinted with multiply. Dot jitter, solid dropout, knock-outs, stepped light pools, and tapered linework that boils at 3 Hz from three seeded bakes.
Any number
The poster gains a row.
Watch out
The people are a first pass: three-key pose loops, not yet walking paths. Faces need sharper re-baking at close zoom. Each room deserves its own illustration round.
